Questions landlords ask before they instruct us.

Straight answers. If yours is not here, ring and ask.

How quickly is rent paid to me?

Within five working days of the tenant's payment clearing, with a statement showing rent, our fee and any repair costs you have approved. You are never waiting on a monthly batch run.

What happens if the tenant stops paying?

We contact the tenant the day rent is missed, agree a plan where one is realistic, and serve the correct notice under the Section 8 grounds where it is not. If possession proceedings are needed, we prepare the file and work alongside your solicitor or our litigation-support partner.

Do I approve repairs?

Yes, above an agreed float, which most landlords set between £150 and £300. Below it we fix the problem and tell you. Above it you get a quote and a recommendation, and we check the work once it is done.

Can I move to you mid-tenancy?

Yes. We take over the file, the deposit registration and the tenant relationship, audit the compliance documents, and tell you what, if anything, is missing. Most handovers complete within a fortnight.

Where do you manage?

Anywhere in England. The office is in Leyton, East London, and the portfolio we manage runs from London and the Home Counties through the Midlands to Yorkshire and the North East. Local trades and inspection visits are arranged wherever the property is.

One agent for the whole of England.

The office is in London. The properties are wherever our landlords bought them: a flat in Leyton, a terrace in Rotherham, a house in Cramlington. One manager, one statement, one standard, whatever the postcode.
