What we collect and why

Landlords and prospective landlords

Name, contact details, property addresses, bank details for rent payments, identity documents where anti-money-laundering checks apply, tax status including non-resident landlord scheme information, and correspondence. We use this to provide the services you instruct, to pay you, and to meet our legal obligations. The lawful bases are performance of a contract and legal obligation.

Tenants, applicants and guarantors

Name, contact details, date of birth, identity and right to rent documents, employment and income information, previous landlord references, credit history from a referencing provider, bank details, and correspondence during the tenancy, including repair reports and inspection photographs. We use this to assess an application, to set up and manage a tenancy, to protect a deposit and to meet legal duties such as right to rent checks. The lawful bases are steps taken at your request before a contract, performance of a contract, legal obligation and, for some checks, our legitimate interest in letting responsibly.

Website visitors

This website does not use analytics cookies or tracking. The contact form opens your own email application with the message filled in, and nothing from it is stored on our web server. Our hosting providers record standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security.

The website assistant

What you type into the assistant, and any photographs you add, are sent to our servers so that it can reply. Its answers are written by an artificial intelligence model supplied by Anthropic, which processes the conversation for that purpose. When you ask it to pass something to us, such as a repair report, a tenancy question or a request for a proposal, it emails the details to our team and, if you gave an email address, sends you a copy.

If you report a repair as an existing tenant, the assistant checks the rent payment reference you give against our tenancy records and emails a one-time code to the address we hold for that tenancy. The conversation is kept in your browser until you close the tab and is not kept on our servers afterwards, though Anthropic may hold it for a limited period under its own terms before deleting it. The emails it sends are kept with the tenancy or enquiry they relate to. Please do not type bank details, card numbers or passwords into it. The lawful bases are steps taken at your request, performance of a contract and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

Who we share it with

Referencing and credit providers, deposit protection schemes, contractors carrying out repairs (address and access details only), utility companies and local authorities where a tenancy requires it, our accountants and professional advisers, the Property Redress Scheme if a complaint is referred, HMRC where the law requires, and property portals for marketing. The website assistant relies on service providers acting on our instructions: Vercel (hosting), Anthropic (the language model), Resend (email delivery) and Zoho (our tenancy records). Some of these providers are based in the United States. We do not sell personal data.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it where we no longer need it, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and ask us to restrict processing while a question is resolved. Email admin@surelets.co.uk. We respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
