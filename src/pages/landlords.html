Let only

We find the tenant and set the tenancy up properly. You take it from there.

9.6% of the first year's rent, inc. VAT

Marketing, viewings, referencing, right to rent checks, a compliant tenancy agreement, deposit registration and the statutory documents the tenant must be given at the start. You receive a complete file and the keys.

Because Section 21 no longer exists, getting the paperwork right at the start matters more than it used to. That is what you are paying for.

Portfolios, blocks and HMOs

Ten units need a system. We run one.

Licensing schedules by council, inspection cycles, certificate expiry across every unit, arrears reporting by property and one monthly statement for the lot. We manage houses in multiple occupation, converted blocks and mixed portfolios anywhere in England.

Portfolio fees are agreed per instruction. Tell us what you have and we will put a figure in writing.
