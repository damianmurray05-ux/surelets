Your email signature.

Fill in your details, press copy, paste into Outlook. The logo animates once when the email is opened, the buttons work, and the legal footer is already in place.

Putting it into Outlook.

Once per device. Outlook on your phone only takes plain text, so use the text version there.

Outlook on Mac or Windows, and Outlook on the web

  1. Press Copy signature above.
  2. In Outlook open Settings (the gear), then Accounts, then Signatures.
  3. Choose New signature, name it Sure Lets, click into the box and paste.
  4. Under Select default signatures set both New messages and Replies and forwards to Sure Lets.
  5. Save. Send yourself a test email and open it on your phone too.

Outlook on iPhone or Android

  1. Open Settings, tap your Sure Lets account, then Signature.
  2. Paste the text version below. Turn on Per account signature if you have more than one account.

What is in it, and why.

    

    

      
    
        
  • The animated logo is a GIF hosted on this site, so it needs no attachment and plays once in Gmail, Apple Mail, Outlook on the web and Outlook on Mac. Older Outlook on Windows shows the first frame, which is the finished logo.
    • 
        
  • The buttons are real links: a proposal request for landlords, the repair reporter for tenants, and a contact card that saves our number and address to their phone.
    • 
        
  • The footer carries the disclosures a limited company has to make on business email, the redress scheme and ICO registrations, and a short confidentiality line. It links to the privacy notice.
    • 
        
  • No web fonts. Email clients ignore them, so the text is set in Helvetica or Arial and the brand font lives in the logo image.
