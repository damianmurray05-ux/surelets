Sure Lets & Manage brand guidelines.

Version 1.0, September 2026. Everything needed to reproduce the identity consistently: the mark, the palette, the type, the voice and the photography. Downloadable files are at the end.

The idea.

A managing agent that is also a landlord. Everything in the identity says "careful, capable, ours": a quiet ink ground, one confident accent, generous space, and type with enough character to feel owned rather than templated.

InkThe weight of the brand. Near-black navy, never pure black.
CobaltOne accent, used for the ampersand, the L's foot, links and emphasis. Never for large areas.
PaperA cool off-white ground that keeps photography and ink crisp.

The mark.

An SL monogram in a rounded tile, set in Bricolage Grotesque, the same face as the wordmark. The S is paper, the L is cobalt: two letters, two colours, nothing added. The tile keeps it legible at 16 pixels and confident at a metre wide. The straplined versions below are banners for letterheads, signage and social headers, not the logo itself.

Rules

  • Clear space around the lockup is the height of the tile on every side. Nothing sits inside it.
  • Minimum sizes: tile 20px on screen, 6mm in print; primary lockup 140px wide on screen, 35mm in print.
  • The ampersand in the wordmark is always cobalt (or pale cobalt on ink) and always weight 400.
  • Never recolour the mark, add effects, rotate it, stretch it, put it in a circle, or set the wordmark in another typeface.
  • On photography, use the reversed lockup over a darkened area, or the ink lockup on a light area. If neither reads cleanly, add the ink tile behind the wordmark.

Colour.

Six colours do everything. Ink and paper carry the brand; cobalt is the only accent. Contrast between text and ground meets WCAG AA at every pairing listed.

Proportions on a typical page: paper 70%, ink 25%, cobalt under 5%. If cobalt starts to feel like a brand colour rather than a highlight, there is too much of it.

Typography.

Two typefaces, both open licensed and self hosted, so the site never depends on a third party.

Display

Bricolage Grotesque

Headlines, numerals and the wordmark. Weight 500 for headlines, 600 for sub-headings and the wordmark. Optical size 96 at display sizes. Tracking minus 2 to 3 percent. Sentence case, always, with a full stop on statements.

Text

Manrope

Body copy, navigation, buttons, tables and forms. Weight 400 for text, 600 for labels and buttons. Line height 1.6 for reading, 1.4 for captions. Never letter-spaced except in eyebrows.

Voice.

We write the way a good property manager talks to a landlord they respect.

Plain, specific, unhurried

Short sentences. Real numbers with VAT included. The name of the form, the number of days, the fee. No adjectives doing the work a fact should do.

Say what we do, not what we are

"Rent paid within five working days" rather than "fast, reliable payments". "We serve the correct Section 8 notice" rather than "expert legal support".

Confident, not salesy

No exclamation marks. No "seamless", "hassle-free", "bespoke" or "elevate". One call to action per page, always "Request a proposal".

Punctuation

Full stops on headlines. Commas and full stops instead of dashes. Ampersand only in the company name. Numbers as figures except at the start of a sentence.

Photography.

Real buildings and real rooms, daylight, straight verticals. Streets from pavement height, interiors from a doorway. No lifestyle models, no stock handshakes, no renders.

Photographs sit in a frame: an outer shell one tone darker than the ground with a hairline, and the image inside with a smaller concentric radius. On dark sections the frame lightens instead. Never a drop shadow, never a border in colour.

Shape and motion.

Shape

  • Buttons are pills
  • Cards and photographs are 20px radius
  • Inputs are 12px radius
  • Hairlines at 12% ink, never solid grey borders
  • Shadows tinted ink, soft, only on hover or floating elements

Motion

  • Content rises 1.6rem and fades in as it enters, once
  • Photographs settle from a 6% zoom
  • Buttons drift a few pixels toward the pointer and spring back
  • Cards carry a faint light that follows the pointer
  • All of it switches off under reduced motion

Layout

  • Asymmetric: 5/7 and 6/6 splits, one featured cell in a grid of three
  • Section headers stack; they never split headline and explainer side by side
  • Space is the main ornament: 4.5 to 9rem between sections
  • One marquee per page at most
  • Single column below 768px
